# YouTube Trending Video Analysis
**Timeline:** Apr 2025

## Project Overview
Analyzed international YouTube trending video statistics to uncover insights on viewer behavior, content popularity, and engagement patterns across different categories.

## Key Tasks & Achievements
- Collected and cleaned video data using **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib)**, and **MS Excel**.
- Visualized key trends including content category performance, likes-to-view ratios, and optimal posting times with **Tableau** and **MS Excel**.
- Conducted **statistical analysis** to compare engagement metrics across categories and constructed **95% confidence intervals** to validate insights.
- Provided actionable recommendations for digital content strategy based on viewer interaction patterns.

## Tools & Technologies
Python, MS Excel, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib

## YouTube data questions
- YouTube data questions:
- What are the top 10 video categories?
- What is the distribution of each video type?
- Which type of video gets the most likes/dislikes?
- Which type of video gets the most comments?
- Which type of video gains the most views?

## Key Insights for Content Creators
- Trending Categories: Entertainment dominates with 32.9% of content, followed by
-- News & Politics (10.2%)
-- People & Blogs (10.0%)
-- Comedy (9.2%)
-- Music (9.1%)
- Optimal Publishing Time: The most common release period is 15:00–22:00, with peak viewing windows at 4:00–5:00 and 13:00–17:00.
- Engagement by Category:
-- Music and Non-profit & Activism receive the most likes
-- Non-profit & Activism also attracts the highest number of dislikes
- Engagement Metrics:
-- The average like-to-view ratio is 4.86% (95% CI: 4.74%–4.98%) → roughly 1 in 20 viewers likes a video.
-- Pets & Animals content generates significantly higher comment rates (9.06) compared to Sports (3.50), with strong statistical significance (t = 11.99, p < 0.001).
## Strategic Insights and Recommendations
- Timing Strategy:
-- Publish videos between 15:00–22:00 to align with audience activity
-- Prioritize promotions between 13:00–17:00 for maximum visibility during peak viewing hours
- Content Strategy:
-- Focus on Entertainment, Comedy, and People & Blogs
-- Integrate music-style elements (rhythm, emotional tone, catchiness) to enhance engagement
- Audience Engagement:
-- Use the 5% like-to-view ratio as a performance benchmark
-- Videos exceeding this ratio indicate above-average audience resonance
- Category-Specific Strategy:
-- Pets & Animals: Leverage strong comment culture with storytelling, user-generated content, and community interaction
-- Sports: Stimulate discussion with predictions, debates, and interactive prompts to raise engagement
-- Non-profit & Activism: Reduce high dislike rates by using narrative-driven storytelling and positive framing instead of direct appeals
